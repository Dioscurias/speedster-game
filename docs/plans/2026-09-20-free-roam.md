# Free Roam Implementation Plan

**Goal:** Replace the lane runner with a faster, unrestricted city exploration game, as requested and clarified by the user.
**Architecture:** A pure 2D world-space locomotion simulation drives a third-person character. Reusable city tiles form a continuous grid of streets, intersections, sidewalks and building blocks. The camera can orbit independently, and WASD movement follows its orientation. Rendering uses nearby tile groups, while collision coordinates remain stable.
**Tech Stack:** Existing Three.js/Vite app, downloaded Kenney models, Node tests and Playwright.

## Requested design
No checkpoints, timer, finish line, automatic forward movement or lateral lane constraint. WASD/arrows moves forward, backward, sideways and diagonally; drag the scene to aim movement in any direction. Release movement to stop. Normal speed 90 m/s (324 km/h), boost 260 m/s (936 km/h). Shift boosts, Space jumps, E slows time. A compass/minimap shows intersections and position. Traffic and building collisions prevent clipping, including while boosting. Pickups replenish and respawn. Stats record distance, top speed and pickups; pause/resume/restart remain.

## Steps
1. Replace old race tests with free-roam tests: four directions, diagonals, arbitrary camera headings, stop, high speed, collision substeps, no endpoint, pause and energy.
2. Implement src/game.js and verify tests.
3. Replace the linear scene with pooled city tiles, world-space traffic and pickups, directional trails, camera orbit and minimap in src/main.js.
4. Update index.html, styles, guide, records and README to reflect free roam.
5. Update browser test; validate movement along both world axes, reverse, orbit, boost, pause, touch controls, collision and asset loading; build and review.
