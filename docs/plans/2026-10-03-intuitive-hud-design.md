# Intuitive HUD Visual Design

The game keeps its Space Grotesk, DM Sans, and Barlow Condensed typography. The interface uses charcoal and smoke glass as its foundation, warm ivory for text, muted gold for active controls, and crimson only for damage, danger, and small speedster accents.

The minimap moves from the information sidebar into the game viewport. It becomes a compact translucent HUD module with the current street and an expand icon. Roads and landmarks remain readable while the map background allows the city scene to show through.

The page removes redundant explanatory prose, difficulty decoration, energy tips, momentum advice, and the persistent keyboard explanation strip. The opening card retains one action and a concise control cue. Optional details remain available through the Field Guide.

Multiplayer speedsters share the same model, materials, gold trim, dark accessories, and skin tone. A stable player-ID palette changes only the primary suit fabric, so each player remains visually distinct after reconnecting without changing human features.

Success means the game view carries the essential information at a glance: speed, health, online players, energy, location, and map. The wider page feels calmer, and multiplayer avatars are distinguishable without looking artificial.
