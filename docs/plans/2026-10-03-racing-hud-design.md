# Full-Screen Racing HUD Design

The game uses the supplied racing screenshot as its layout reference. The 3D world fills the browser viewport. Interface elements float over the game only where they provide immediate driving information.

Player count and health sit at the top left with compact run statistics below. The minimap becomes circular and moves to the top right. Speed and energy sit at the bottom right. Location stays at the bottom left. Camera and power buttons remain reachable without becoming a separate toolbar.

The website header, page title, sidebar dashboard, footer, and surrounding page margins are removed from the active layout. Start, pause, death, guide, and records appear as overlays over the world. The pause screen uses the direct labels Paused, Resume, and Restart.

Desktop uses fixed overlay positions. Mobile keeps the same full-screen model while moving controls away from the minimap and preserving touch movement.
